FLUX is a reseller. This page explains what that means for you.

## 1. Where products come from

The products you see in FLUX are supplied by an **independent third-party supplier** that provides
digital goods through its reseller API. We buy from that supplier and sell to you. The supplier's
name is available on request through support.

## 2. What we add

- We set the price you pay. It is the supplier's price plus our margin, so it can be higher than the
  price you would pay the brand directly.
- We handle your payment, your wallet, your order record, refunds and support.
- We pass the supplier's delivery details to you under Orders.

## 3. What we do not control

- The **quality, validity and continued availability** of the product are controlled by the supplier
  and the brand that owns the service.
- Stock can change in seconds. A product shown as in stock may be sold out when you buy it.
- The supplier's **own terms** can apply to the product, such as account rules, regional limits,
  or how long a code stays valid. You must follow those terms.

## 4. Our relationship with brands

FLUX is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by** the brands whose names appear in our
catalogue. Brand names and logos belong to their owners and are used only to describe the product.

## 5. If a supplier problem affects your order

We will tell you what we know and what happens next, following the [Refund Policy](refund-policy.md).
Where the supplier can replace a faulty product, we will ask them to do so. If the supplier cannot,
we refund your wallet where the refund policy allows.

## 6. Changes

We may change suppliers or the products we offer. Existing orders stay with the supplier that filled
them.
