# Refund Policy

**{{COMPANY_NAME}}** · **Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains when you get your money back. It applies together with the
[Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). Nothing here limits refund rights you have under the law.

## 1. Quick answers

| Situation | What happens | Where the money goes |
|---|---|---|
| The supplier rejected your order before it was placed (for example, supplier out of stock or supplier balance too low) | Automatic refund | Your FLUX wallet |
| We could not confirm the order with the supplier and we find no record it was placed | Refund after our check | Your FLUX wallet |
| A product you bought was never delivered and the supplier confirms it was not placed | Refund | Your FLUX wallet |
| The delivered product does not work because of a supplier fault | Replacement from the supplier if available; otherwise refund | Replacement, or your FLUX wallet |
| You were charged twice for one order | Refund of the duplicate | Your FLUX wallet |
| You bought the wrong product or plan by mistake, and the product has **not** been delivered | Case-by-case; we will cancel and refund if the supplier allows it | Your FLUX wallet |
| You changed your mind after delivery | Not refundable (digital goods are "used" once delivered) | — |
| Delivered details were shared by you with others and then stopped working | Not refundable | — |
| You have a wallet balance you have not used | Refund on request within 7 days of the deposit (see section 4) | Original payment method, minus network costs |

## 2. Order refunds go to your wallet

Refunds for orders are returned to your **FLUX wallet balance**, so you can use them for another
purchase. We do not pay order refunds in cash.

## 3. How to ask for a refund

1. Open **Profile → Help & support** and choose the order.
2. Include the **order ID** (shown under Orders), what happened, and any screenshot that helps.
3. We reply within {{REFUND_RESPONSE_DAYS}} business days. For orders that need checking with the
   supplier, we tell you when we expect a result.

Please do not open a second request for the same order. Duplicates slow down the review.

## 4. Deposits (top-ups)

- A deposit that has not been used can be refunded on request within 7 days of the deposit being
  credited. The refund goes back to the payment method you used, minus any network or transfer
  costs that were charged to us.
- A deposit sent to the wrong address, or with the wrong amount, may take longer to find. We will
  tell you what we need from you.
- A deposit that has been spent on products is not refundable as cash.

## 5. Orders that stay "needs review"

Some orders take longer because the supplier's answer was unclear. FLUX does the following:

- We check the supplier's own order records and decide from them.
- If we find the order was placed, it is delivered, and you do not need to do anything.
- If we find the order was not placed, we refund your wallet.
- If we find no record and cannot resolve it, a staff member reviews the case and decides, and
  we tell you the outcome.

We will not refund an order automatically without that check, because the supplier may already
have processed it. We also will not leave an order unresolved indefinitely.

## 6. Chargebacks and disputes

Please contact us before you file a dispute with your bank or payment provider. Payment disputes
on a delivered digital product, opened without contacting us first, may lead us to suspend the
account while we investigate.

## 7. Exceptions

We cannot refund an order when:

- the delivered details were used, shared or changed in a way that breaks the product's terms;
- you ask for a refund after the period stated in section 1 for the situation;
- the problem is caused by the underlying provider's policy changes after delivery.

## 8. Changes to this policy

Changes apply to orders placed after the new effective date.
