# Privacy Policy

**{{COMPANY_NAME}}** · **Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains what personal data FLUX uses, why, and what choices you have.

## 1. Data we collect

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram user ID, username and first name | Telegram, when you open FLUX | To identify your account, show your name, and send you messages through the bot |
| Wallet balance and transaction history (deposits, purchases, refunds) | Created by your use of FLUX | To run your wallet and prove what was charged |
| Deposit details: method, amount, reference, transaction reference you submit | You, when you deposit | To match your payment and credit your wallet |
| Orders: product, plan, price, status, and any delivery details | Created when you buy | To deliver products and answer questions about them |
| Support conversations | You, through the bot or support chat | To answer your questions and keep a record of what was decided |
| Basic technical records (requests and errors) | Our servers | To keep FLUX running and to fix faults |

We do not collect your phone number, address, or payment-card details. Telegram itself handles your
phone number and contact list, and we do not receive them.

## 2. How we use data

- To run your account and wallet, and to process orders and deposits.
- To place orders with our supplier. **The supplier receives only the product and quantity you
  ordered, not your name, Telegram ID or other personal details.**
- To send you order updates and support replies through the Telegram bot.
- To detect fraud, prevent abuse, and keep records we need for accounting and legal reasons.
- To improve FLUX, using aggregated information that does not identify you.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 3. Who we share data with

- **Telegram**, which delivers messages and sign-in between you and FLUX.
- **Our hosting and database providers**, which store FLUX data on our behalf.
- **Our supplier**, only the product and quantity needed to fulfil an order (see section 2).
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

## 4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed database. Access is limited to staff who need it to run the service, and
admin actions are restricted to the Telegram accounts we have approved. Sign-in uses Telegram's own
signed session data, which we verify on every request. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so
please keep your Telegram account protected, for example with two-step verification.

## 5. How long we keep data

We keep order, deposit and ledger records for {{DATA_RETENTION}}, because we need them for
accounting, refunds and legal purposes. Support conversations are kept for the same period. When the
period ends, we delete or anonymise the records, except where the law requires us to keep them longer.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- see the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct data that is wrong;
- ask us to delete data, subject to records we must keep;
- object to or restrict some uses;
- receive a copy of your data in a usable format.

Contact us through the support chat to make a request. We will confirm your identity through your
Telegram account before we act on it, and we will answer within the time the law requires.

## 7. Cookies and device storage

The app stores your appearance choice (light, dark or Telegram theme) on your device. It does not use
advertising cookies. Telegram may store its own data on your device.

## 8. Children

FLUX is not intended for children under the age required to enter a contract where you live. If we
learn that we have data about a child without proper permission, we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date when we change this policy and tell you about material changes
through the bot.
